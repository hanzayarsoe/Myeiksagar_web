Why Myeiksagar exists

Bridging standard Burmese and the Myeik dialect with NLP tools built on campus.

In Myanmar’s Myeik region, everyday speech often differs from the standard Burmese taught in schools and used online. Friends, markets, and family conversations carry local words that dictionaries rarely list.

ဘိတ်စကား (Myeiksagar) သည် စံမြန်မာနှင့် မြိတ်ဒေသစကားအကြား စကားလုံးနှင့် စာကြောင်း ဘာသာပြန် ကူညီရန် University of Computer Studies (Myeik) မှ ကျောင်းသားများ တည်ဆောက်ထားသော စီမံကိန်းဖြစ်သည်။

The system looks up phrases in a growing community dictionary, then falls back to word-level composition. A CRF segmenter helps split Burmese text. Missing words can be contributed through Insert Data — so the corpus grows with the region.

  • Mission

    Make Myeik dialect phrases easier to look up, learn, and share — for students, visitors, and speakers at home.

  • Vision

    Keep regional speech visible in digital tools, not only in spoken life around the Andaman coast.

  • Tools

    Phrase translation, CRF segmentation, a culture quiz, and a community dictionary collected with Insert Data.

Meet the team

Students and mentors behind Myeiksagar.

Myeiksagar grew out of coursework and local curiosity: how do you teach a machine words that live mainly in conversation? The team paired dictionary collection with simple NLP pipelines so the site stays useful even as the lexicon is still growing.
